# THE WORLD TODAY — Sunday 22 November 2026

A classic broadsheet front page summing up the world's news of Sunday
22 November 2026: dense serif columns, ruled rules, a lead story, a
numbers strip, a pinned map, and a date-check footer. 30 stories, every
one first reported on 22 November 2026.

## The day in one page

- **Lead — 2 tankers hit by mines off Hormuz (17:13 UTC).** Iran's navy
  said two supertankers struck naval mines and caught fire east of the
  Strait of Hormuz, north of Ghanam Island. (Iran navy · AP)
- **No deal at COP31.** The Antalya summit ended with no agreement after
  the EU and about 130 parties rejected a text that never named fossil
  fuels — the longest climate conference ever, the first to fail since
  2009. (CNN · Reuters)
- **258 — Democrats win the House.** Hubbard beat Foxx by 330 votes in a
  North Carolina recount, giving Democrats their largest House majority
  since 1992. Republicans hold 173; four races uncalled. (AP · Reuters)

## How to read it

- **REPORTED** = confirmed by 2+ sources · **CLAIM** = one side alleges,
  unverified · **ONGOING** = still developing.
- **NOT YET — do not read as done:** 4 House races uncalled; COP32
  venue not set; Gaza ceasefire talks with no deal announced; the Iranian
  tanker convoy has not yet tested the blockade.
- **Date check:** every story above was first reported on 22 November
  2026. Excluded on purpose: El Chapo Isidro captured 20 Nov; BepiColombo
  Mercury orbit 21 Nov; ICJ Myanmar ruling 19 Nov; Wall St rout 20 Nov;
  FBI NCIC hack first reported 21 Nov. The Banten toll used here is the
  22 November first report (3 dead, 36 missing), not the later toll.
